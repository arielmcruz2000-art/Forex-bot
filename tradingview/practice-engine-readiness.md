# Practice engine readiness

The pure evaluator `server/practice-intent.mjs` prepares research entry intents from verified complete EUR_USD M15 midpoint candles. It does not contact a broker, produce an executable order payload, run on a schedule or persist trading state. Routing remains disabled. Its tests use fabricated fixtures, not historical profitability evidence.

Frozen rules: SMA5/20 crossunder only, signal closes on weekdays 07:00 inclusive–12:00 exclusive New York, October 12–December 12 UTC test window, one position, 1% research equity sizing, 20-pip stop/60-pip target and 4% daily entry latch. Cost-aware sizing matches the Pine proxy of 0.0001 USD per unit per side and two ticks per fill (EURUSD tick assumed 0.00001). Margin sizing uses the Pine 2% model and 5% reserve. These proxies do not replace actual spread, financing or broker margin rules.

Use a separate 1000 USD research ledger to compare with Pine. The demo account balance is not that ledger and account-wide P/L is not attributable to the bot. Callers must provide reconciled daily baseline, latched lock, exposure and last signal key. Missing or outdated state fails closed. A new NY day requires a deliberate reconciled state rollover; equity recovery must never clear the latch within a day. Stale signals are rejected after 90 seconds from close. Repeated candidates must be recorded atomically before any future submission. This guard alone is not durable deduplication.

Before enabling demo orders, implement and test: durable atomic signal claims; broker transaction reconciliation on restart and timeout; bid/ask and margin checks; stale-price and spread limits; actual-fill anchored protective brackets; practice-only endpoint guards and a kill switch; separate bot trade accounting including financing. Lost responses must be reconciled before retrying, and existing foreign/manual account exposure must block entries. Protective exits must survive an entry pause. No route or enable flag for order execution is added in this change.

Validate the frozen TradingView forward study first, preserving its independent evidence and history completeness checks. No profitable performance claim follows from these engineering tests. Keep artificial Netlify prices, Pine broker-emulator recalculations, OANDA market data and any future demo executions in separate records. Never export private account metrics to GitHub without explicit authorization.
